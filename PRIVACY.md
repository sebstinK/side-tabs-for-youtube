# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 9 November 2026*

**Side Tabs for YouTube does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

## What the extension does

It runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/*` and rearranges elements that are already on the page into tabs in the right sidebar. It does not read your account, your watch history or the content of the videos.

## Data stored

The extension stores one value in your browser using the `chrome.storage.local` API: the name of the last tab you selected (for example `comments`). This value never leaves your device and is deleted when you remove the extension.

## Network requests

The extension makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics, no tracking and no remotely loaded code.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Remember which tab you selected last. |
| Content script on `www.youtube.com` | Show the tabs on YouTube watch pages. |

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
